Variable popover: hover tooltip + side inspector (alternative)

Hover shows a read-only tooltip (value and winning scope). Click a mark, or F2 with the caret on it, opens a Variable inspector docked at the right of the request pane, with the full scope chain. Enter saves the current value of the winning scope, Esc closes the inspector and returns focus to the field.
