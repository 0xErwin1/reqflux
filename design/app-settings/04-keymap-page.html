Keymap page

Laid out as DBFlux's Keyboard shortcuts page (design/app-settings: DBFlux settings/keybindings.rs): a filter and a context select above the list, rows grouped by context under a header that folds, one keycap per chord part, a pencil that records the next chord. The [keymap] block of config.toml is a collapsed line at the foot instead of a large input-like box.

Filter, capture and conflict

The filter matches the command label, its slug or the key as printed, and opens every group. The pencil (or a click on the keycaps) listens for the next chord; a chord another command holds raises the conflict banner above the list.
